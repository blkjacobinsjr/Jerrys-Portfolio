# Enter screen sound: expert panel (simulated)

This is a **simulated** review. No real experts were consulted. Each of the 10 roles below stands for a sound-design
discipline. I wrote the strongest objection that discipline would raise, then answered it with a measurement, a test
or a reason. Everything was measured on 24.10.2026 on the box: headless Chromium/WebKit with software GL, plus ffmpeg
and EBU R128 analysis. The panel ran 2 rounds, the maximum. Round 1 found the 55 ms and 391 ms click latencies, the
offset drop transient, the silent head on the swell and the AAC padding. Round 2 confirmed the fixes.

Scope: the name sting (apple-music-effect oil lens) on desktop, the "Enter with sound" click (Cuelume `tap`, plus the
ElevenLabs oil drop and air swell), and the ElevenLabs jazz bed. Phones get the sounds but not the lens.

| # | Role | Highest-leverage objection | Test that decides it | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Interaction-audio engineer (latency) | A click sound that lands late reads as lag, not polish. | Instrument `AudioBufferSourceNode.start`/`OscillatorNode.start` against the click's `timeStamp` (e2e + capture.mjs). | **Resolved** by decoding on intent (hover/focus/keydown, OfflineAudioContext) and scheduling synchronously in the click. Event→first sound: 55 ms (r1) → 391 ms when the leave work ran first → 26–47 ms (r2). Handler→start is 3–12 ms on Chromium desktop, and the e2e gate is <50 ms. The remaining input delay is the software-GL frame (~66–80 ms per frame headless). |
| 2 | UI-sound system designer (Cuelume) | Two sound systems on one click turns into mud and owns nothing clearly. | Read Cuelume's docs: what job maps to which cue, and when to unlock. | **Resolved**: Cuelume plays the button's UI cue (`tap`, which its docs give for a primary button) and `unlock()` runs inside the click as they ask. It is imported on intent so both happen in the gesture. ElevenLabs owns the scene: drop, swell and bed. Measured: Cuelume oscillators start 48 ms after the click and the drop 51 ms. |
| 3 | Film sound editor (sync) | The swell has to peak with the picture, not near it. | Measure the RMS peak and the transient position in the trimmed files against the 1.1 s photo rise. | **Resolved**: the drop transient is 2.9 ms into the file (it was 336 ms). The swell's RMS peak is at 512 ms and plays at +60 ms, so it peaks about 570 ms after the click, against a rise midpoint of about 550 ms. |
| 4 | Music editor (loops) | A 28.5 s loop repeats until it grates, and the seam clicks. | Measure the seam jump against the signal's p99 sample delta for each codec, and count the passes. | **Resolved**: a 1.5 s equal-power crossfade from tail into head. Seam jump is 0.018 (webm) and 0.024 (m4a) against p99 0.020. The m4a decodes 64 near-silent padding samples (RMS 0.005 against 0.066), so `loopEnd` is clamped to 28.5 s. The bed plays 3 passes (about 85 s), then fades out for good. |
| 5 | Mix engineer (loudness) | Music under a portfolio has to sit underneath, not over the reading. | Check EBU R128 on the files and the runtime gain. | **Resolved**: jazz is -18.0 LUFS and -1.9 dBTP, played at a bed gain of 0.32 (about -10 dB, roughly -28 LUFS effective). It drops to ×0.35 under the footer's city ambience, follows the mute toggle, and fades out when the tab is hidden. Short SFX peak at -6 dBFS. |
| 6 | Accessibility audio specialist | Sound and motion that start unasked hurt people. | Check autoplay and reduced-motion behaviour in e2e. | **Resolved**: nothing sounds before the click, and "Enter without sound" stays one tab-stop away. Jazz is never requested before the click (e2e asserts it). The lens is off under `prefers-reduced-motion` and on coarse pointers (e2e asserts no canvas on phones). |
| 7 | Game-audio designer (feedback on hover) | The button's lens on hover should sound too. Silent motion feels dead. | Check whether the browser allows it and what Cuelume says. | **Rebutted**: audio can't start before a user gesture, and hover doesn't count as one. Cuelume's docs also say "never play a cue … on hover". The hover is visual-only, and the sound is saved for the commitment. |
| 8 | Brand sonic designer (the name sting) | Apple's sting is a picture-and-sound unit. A silent name reveal is half a logo. | Same autoplay check. | **Rebutted**: the name plays on load, before any gesture, so any sound there would be blocked or would need a console-warning workaround. The sonic logo moves to the click instead: drop, then swell, then the bed. |
| 9 | Generative-audio producer (AI artefacts) | AI SFX smear: double hits, tails, noise beds. | Listen to and look at the spectrograms (audio/spec/all.png), and count transients. | **Resolved** by selection and trimming: 2 takes per SFX. drop-b (messy) and swell-b (a boomy -11 LUFS drone) were rejected. drop-a had several plops and was trimmed to one (0.324–0.624 s). Prompts carry no artist or song names. The Music API returned 402 (paid plan), so the bed is an SFX v3 `loop:true` generation. |
| 10 | Web-audio performance engineer | 4 s of spectral ray tracing plus audio decode will drop frames or hurt Lighthouse. | Run Lighthouse on the desktop and mobile home, and track frame time in the capture. | **Resolved for the budget**: the lens loads after `load`, only on the desktop gate. It is not in the Lighthouse path (no `?enter=1`). It drops to 0.6× scale after 2 slow frames and stops rendering at the click. Lighthouse results are in the hand-off. The real-GPU frame rate is **not measured** (headless SwiftShader runs at about 15 fps): needs Jerry, one look on his Mac. |
